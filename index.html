Cybersecurity Engineering — ENSA Tangier

Part of the inaugural cohort of the dedicated Cybersecurity Engineering track at ENSA Tangier, I completed an engineering program built on strong foundations in computer science, mathematics, networking, software development and systems engineering.

The curriculum progressively develops advanced expertise in offensive and defensive security, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, cryptography, threat intelligence, malware analysis, digital forensics and security governance.
